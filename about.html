Our story and direction

Built on care. Designed for change.

SKYRENZA is an India-based company being developed with an international outlook and a focus on customer experience, business operations, IT support and responsible AI adoption.

Founder & vision

Abhijit Kale

Founder, SKYRENZA

Abhijit Kale founded SKYRENZA with the ambition of building an outsourcing partner that can help businesses serve their customers and manage operations with care, consistency and clear communication.

The company's long-term vision is shaped by the changing world of work: customers expect fast, helpful service; businesses need reliable processes; and AI is creating new opportunities to support teams and simplify repetitive work.

SKYRENZA aims to connect these needs through a practical model that values skilled people, process discipline, learning and thoughtful technology adoption.

“Our ambition is to build a company clients can rely on—and a workplace where people can keep learning and growing.”
Abhijit Kale

Founder · SKYRENZA
