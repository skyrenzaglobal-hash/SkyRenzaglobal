Inspired by modern support platforms

Learn from the market. Design for the client.

Platforms such as Pylon show how conversations, customer context, knowledge, AI agents, integrations and reporting can be connected in one support workflow. Large CX providers also emphasize the balance between automation and expert human service.

SKYRENZA can use these patterns as a reference when recommending a client's tool stack, workflows and operating model. The right solution may use existing software rather than build a new platform from scratch.

  • Map customer channels and current systems.
  • Identify repetitive work and common support intents.
  • Choose low-risk use cases with clear success measures.
  • Test accuracy, privacy, escalation and agent experience.
  • Scale only when results and controls are acceptable.

Responsible AI

Trust is part of the design.

AI in customer operations needs controls that match the data, use case and consequences of an incorrect answer.

Data minimization

Use only the information necessary for the defined task and respect the client's retention and access rules.

Accuracy & transparency

Validate important answers against trusted sources and make it clear when a response needs human confirmation.

Escalation & monitoring

Define when automation stops, who reviews exceptions and how errors or risks are reported.

No AI feature is represented as active or production-ready unless it has been implemented, tested and connected to the necessary systems.
