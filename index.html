About SKYRENZA

A practical outsourcing partner built around your business.

SKYRENZA is an India-based BPO and Customer Experience company focused on helping growing businesses build reliable, scalable and people-focused customer operations. We believe outsourcing should be more than transferring tasks: it should create a dependable extension of the client's team.
