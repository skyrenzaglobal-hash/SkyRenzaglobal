Meet the founder

Abhijit Kale

Founder, SKYRENZA

SKYRENZA was founded with the ambition of building an India-based outsourcing partner for businesses in international markets. The vision is to bring people, disciplined processes and evolving technology together to help organizations serve customers more effectively.

The company’s direction reflects a belief that outsourcing should be more than transferring tasks: it should create a dependable extension of a client's team, with clear communication, thoughtful service and a commitment to learning.

“Build with care. Work with clarity. Improve with every interaction.”

As customer expectations and AI capabilities evolve, SKYRENZA aims to explore practical, responsible ways to help businesses improve customer operations while keeping human judgment where it matters.
