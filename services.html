How an engagement begins

Clear scope before delivery.

Good outsourcing begins with a shared understanding of the problem, the people involved, the tools required and the outcomes that matter.

  • Define channels, volumes and operating hours.
  • Agree service levels, quality measures and escalation paths.
  • Confirm training, access, privacy and security requirements.
  • Choose a pilot or phased launch where appropriate.
  • Review results before expanding the scope.
